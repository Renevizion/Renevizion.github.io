The problem

Good work gets harder to choose when the path is unclear.

01

People lose the thread.

They find you but cannot tell what to do next. They have a question but do not know where to ask it. They want to begin but the process feels like work.

02

The team loses the handoff.

A request comes in but the next person does not have what they need. Someone has to remember. Someone has to chase. The work slows down.

The result is the same: people hesitate and good work takes more effort than it should.
