What I do

Product and system work for ideas that need direction.

Whether the starting point is a new concept, scattered business process, or early product, the goal is the same: turn uncertainty into a defined, usable path forward.

01

Product direction

Clarify the problem, the intended user, and the smallest version of the product worth building.

  • Feature scope
  • Product requirements

02

Workflow & user-flow design

Map how the work happens now, find the points of friction, and create a cleaner path for people and information.

  • Workflow mapping
  • User journeys
  • System handoffs

Prototype & launch planning

Turn the plan into an interface, a prototype, or a build-ready handoff with a realistic way to test what matters.
